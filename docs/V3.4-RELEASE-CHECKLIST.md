## Warning Scope

The single WARN covers the interactive and RF surfaces, which cannot be
exercised over a management link:

- Pager Portal and theme rendering
- Button navigation and the results browser
- Alert delivery on a real `pineapple_client_connected` event
- RF discovery and AP association
- Audio (`RINGTONE`) — not exercised; the LED and haptic paths were, and share
  the same invocation contract

No automated or source-level gate is failing. The release notes expose this
limitation and rollback criteria are documented.

## Research

No new external research was required. This release contains no protocol- or
firmware-dependent changes and was validated directly against firmware 24.10.1
on hardware. `docs/V3.3-RESEARCH.md` remains the current research record and is
intentionally not renamed or duplicated: the research record tracks findings,
not version numbers.
